Read the numbers in context

Month length. August has 31 days and September has 30. Comparisons use displayed monthly values, assuming unchanged GA4 collection, filters, and reporting settings. No daily trends are reconstructed.

Audience. Users overlap across page rows and across report scopes. The dashboard never adds active users or averages engagement across reports. Portfolio view and key-event cards are explicitly the sum of the displayed report totals, with MDS mixed scope included.

Program scope. OMCS uses /online-mcs in August and broader mcs in September; unseen paths may differ. OMDS uses /online-mds in August and broader mds in September, including Professional MDS. An OMDS monthly growth rate is intentionally unavailable.

Conversions. Key events count recorded actions and may repeat. They are not unique leads, completed applications, registrations, or enrollments. Zero key events do not prove no outcomes occurred or that collection is configured.

Search visibility. Snapshot dates and coverage differ. Best positions deduplicate exact terms within each export. Term counts across reports can overlap. Semrush Traffic field sums are estimates, not measured September organic visits. An absent keyword is not assumed lost.

Coverage. Each PDF provides ten visible September page rows. Unseen counts remain unknown; MDS obscured engagement remains unavailable. Some Digital Learning and Responsible AI page URLs are not specified in the attached PDFs and have no invented page link.

PageSpeed. Initial current-page audits are from October 13, 2026. Mobile and desktop results belong to the exact audited URL. Origin-level Chrome field data describes the origin rather than the individual page. Current audits do not reconstruct September performance.
